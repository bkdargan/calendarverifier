# Airbnb + Vrbo Sync Checker

A free tool that compares Airbnb and Vrbo iCal calendars to detect:

- Mismatched bookings  
- Missing blocks  
- Double‑booking risks  
- Sync delays  
- Checkout‑day conflicts  

## How to Use
1. Download your Airbnb iCal  
2. Download your Vrbo iCal  
3. Upload both files  
4. View mismatches and risk zones  

## Deploy on Netlify
- Build command: none  
- Publish directory: `/`
